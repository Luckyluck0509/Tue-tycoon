TU/e Tycoon is an incremental university simulator inspired by the Computer Science & Engineering program at TU Eindhoven.

Start your journey as a first-year student and earn ECTS by studying, attending lectures, and completing assignments. As your progress grows, you can rebirth to advance to the next quartile, unlocking new challenges and opportunities while keeping permanent bonuses.

Your ultimate goal is to earn 60 ECTS within 4 quartiles and secure a positive Binding Study Advice (BSA). Manage your time wisely, optimize your study strategy, and see how efficiently you can progress through the academic year.

Can you survive the TU/e grind and make it to the end of the year?
